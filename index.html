Hello, I'm

Jiaxun 加勋 Liu 刘

PhD student at Duke University

I'm fortunate to be advised by Professor Boyuan Chen at the General Robotics Lab

I'm interested in computational robot embodiment design, exploring how a robot's morphology can sense and adapt based on task demands to enrich versatility, facilitate robot learning, and enhance task performance. I like to think outside the box and seek unconventional solutions with a full-stack mindset.

Imagine if AI and robots take over our labor, what will we do? We enjoy the arts and investigate deeper questions! Outside research, I’m drawn to design at the intersection of art, fashion, and robotics. If you’re interested in bringing robots into product design, interactive art, or film, I’d love to collaborate.
